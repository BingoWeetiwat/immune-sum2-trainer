How to mark yourself. The real paper awards the marks on the written answer — the scientific name and the stage, plus the key points. For entomology, medical importance must be written as “Vector of …”, “Direct injury …” or “Intermediate host of …”; a bare disease name scores nothing. Mark yourself Partial if you got the organism but missed the stage or a key point.
